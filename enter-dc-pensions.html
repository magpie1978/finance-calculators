Private Pensions / Defined Contribution Pensions

These are pensions that you, your employer, or both pay into from your salary. They are called "defined contribution pensions" because you pay in a set amount, this is invested by the pension company, and you use this money in retirement by either buying an annuity, or simply withdrawing cash from the pension when needed.
